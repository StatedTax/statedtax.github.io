Privacy Policy for Precision Fail

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Precision Fail (“the app”) is developed by MitroGames (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, who handles it, and the choices you have.

The short version

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information ourselves. The app has no accounts, no login, and no servers of our own. Your game progress stays on your device. The only third parties involved are Google, through advertising and in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

Your progress — best round, coins, unlocked themes, owned items, quests and settings — is saved in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID)
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and country
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • information about ads shown, such as impressions, clicks and rewards earned

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us. Google’s handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your advertising choices

Where you are What you are shown and how to change it
European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland The app asks for your consent before personalised advertising is used, via Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the app.
United States You can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising through the notice presented in the app.
Anywhere You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android devices), which limits ad personalisation across all apps.

You can also remove advertising entirely with the one-time No Ads purchase described below.

Purchases

The app offers a single optional purchase, No Ads, which permanently removes banner and interstitial advertising. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or receive your payment card, billing address, or any other payment details — Google handles the entire transaction and only tells the app whether the item is owned.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Progress stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app, or when you use Settings → Reset progress. To request deletion of data held by Google, use the links in the Advertising section above.
